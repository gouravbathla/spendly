About Spendly

Everything you need to know about the app

What is Spendly?

Spendly is a lightweight personal expense tracker that helps you log your spending, understand where your money goes, and stay on top of your budget — without the overhead of a spreadsheet.

Key Features

Instant expense logging — Add an expense in seconds with an amount, category, date, and optional description.

Category breakdowns — See exactly how much you're spending across categories like Food, Transport, Bills, Health, Entertainment, Shopping, and more.

Flexible date filtering — View your spending for this month, the last 3 or 6 months, or any custom date range.

Dashboard summary — Get a quick snapshot of your recent transactions and overall spending at a glance.

Who is it for?

Spendly is built for anyone who wants a simple, no-friction way to track personal expenses — whether you're managing a monthly budget, watching discretionary spending, or just curious where your money goes.

Getting Started

Create a free account, sign in, and start adding expenses right away. No credit card required, and your data stays private to your account.
